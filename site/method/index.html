Method and assumptions

Room lumen budget calculator equation

Delivered lumens = room area × target illuminance; Installed lumen budget = delivered lumens / (utilization × maintenance).

Step-by-step logic

Multiply room area by your target illuminance (1 foot-candle = 1 lumen/ft²; 1 lux = 1 lumen/m²) to get the light needed at the working plane. Divide by utilization times maintenance fraction so the installed bulbs compensate for light absorbed by surfaces and gradual LED lumen depreciation.

Units and rounding

Room dimensions convert at 0.3048 m per foot; 1 foot-candle equals exactly 10.7639104 lux. Lumen totals display rounded to whole lumens.

Limits and edge cases

Room illuminance depends on wall reflectance, ceiling height, fixture beam spread, daylight, and glare control. Split the installed lumen budget across ambient ceiling fixtures and local task lighting rather than one harsh source. This is a planning estimate, not a code-certified lighting design.
