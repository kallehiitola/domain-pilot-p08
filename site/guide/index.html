How to how many lumens do i need

Estimate a total light-output budget from room area and your chosen illuminance target. Include utilization and maintenance assumptions without confusing fixture lumens with light reaching the room’s working plane.

Quick answer: A 20 m² room at 150 lux requires 3,000 lm at the target plane; at 0.70 utilization and 0.90 maintenance, the installed budget is about 4,762 lm.

What you need to measure

  • Room length
  • Room width
  • Target illuminance
  • Utilization fraction
  • Maintenance fraction

How to calculate it

Area multiplied by lux gives lumens reaching the target plane. Divide that by utilization multiplied by maintenance fraction for an estimated installed lumen budget. In US units, one foot-candle is one lumen per square foot.

What changes the estimate

The example illuminance is not a recommendation for every room. Task lighting, surface reflectance, daylight, beam distribution, glare and measurement-plane height all affect a real design. This estimates a lumen budget and does not certify a compliant lighting design.

Worked example

A 20 m² room at 150 lux requires 3,000 lm at the target plane; at 0.70 utilization and 0.90 maintenance, the installed budget is about 4,762 lm.
