How many lumens do I need?

Estimate your total room light-output budget in lumens from floor area, target illuminance (foot-candles or lux), and realistic utilization and maintenance losses.

Quick answer: US living room: a 16 × 12 ft (192 sq ft) room at 15 fc needs 2,880 delivered lm, or about 4,571 installed lm (roughly six 800-lm bulbs). Metric: a 20 m² room at 150 lux requires 3,000 lm on the target plane; at 0.70 utilization and 0.90 maintenance, install about 4,762 lm.

How the calculation works

Multiply room area by your target illuminance (1 foot-candle = 1 lumen/ft²; 1 lux = 1 lumen/m²) to get the light needed at the working plane. Divide by utilization times maintenance fraction so the installed bulbs compensate for light absorbed by surfaces and gradual LED lumen depreciation.

Example 2

US living room: a 16 × 12 ft (192 sq ft) room at 15 fc needs 2,880 delivered lm, or about 4,571 installed lm (roughly six 800-lm bulbs).

What this estimate does not cover

Room illuminance depends on wall reflectance, ceiling height, fixture beam spread, daylight, and glare control. Split the installed lumen budget across ambient ceiling fixtures and local task lighting rather than one harsh source. This is a planning estimate, not a code-certified lighting design.
